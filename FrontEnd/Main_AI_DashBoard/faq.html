Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VEGEGRADE and AI-based vegetable quality assessment.

About VEGEGRADE

VEGEGRADE is a national AI-powered platform that helps farmers, traders, and market officials assess the quality of vegetables using computer vision. The system automatically assigns a grade (A, B, C, or Rotten) based on visual analysis of uploaded images.

All grading and trade data is recorded on the Sepolia blockchain to ensure transparent and tamper-proof records.

VEGEGRADE is currently operational for Tomatoes (Commodity ID: 23). Support for additional vegetables including onion, potato, capsicum, and leafy greens is planned for upcoming releases.

VEGEGRADE is available to:

  • Registered farmers and producer groups
  • Licensed commission agents and traders
  • APMC and mandi officials
  • FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations)

All users must register on the portal with a valid Aadhaar-linked farmer ID or trader license number.

Using the AI Assessment Tool

For best results, follow these practices when uploading images:

  • Photograph vegetables under natural or bright, even lighting - avoid flash glare and deep shadows.
  • Ensure the image is in focus and sharp.
  • Place vegetables on a plain, light-coloured surface (white or cream).
  • Keep the camera directly above the produce (top-down or slight angle).
  • Avoid cluttered backgrounds or mixing multiple produce types in one image.

See our full Upload Guidelines for illustrated instructions.

The system accepts images in JPG, PNG, and WebP formats. The maximum file size is 5 MB. Images larger than 5 MB will be rejected at upload. For best performance, use images with a resolution of at least 800 x 800 pixels.

Under normal server load, AI analysis is completed in 5-15 seconds. During peak hours (9 AM - 12 PM and 3 PM - 6 PM), processing may take up to 30 seconds. Do not refresh the page while analysis is in progress.

The AI model is deterministic - uploading the same image will consistently produce the same result. If you believe a result is inaccurate, consider uploading a better-quality photograph taken under different lighting conditions. For disputes, contact your APMC office.

Grading & Pricing
  • Grade A (Excellent): Premium quality produce with uniform colour, no defects, and optimal freshness. Commands the highest market price.
  • Grade B (Good): Minor surface blemishes or slight colour variation; suitable for retail and direct sale.
  • Grade C (Average): Noticeable defects, irregular size, or reduced freshness. Suitable for processing, juice, or secondary markets.
  • Rotten (Rejected): Significant decay, mould, or severe damage. Not suitable for sale. Rejected by the system.

For detailed grading criteria, visit the Grading Standards page.

Prices displayed on the portal are sourced daily from the CEDA (Commodity Exchange Data Authority) and reflect prevailing wholesale rates at major mandis. These are updated each morning before market opening. The portal does not set or control these prices.

Market prices fluctuate based on supply and demand, seasonal factors, and geographic location. The prices displayed are indicative wholesale rates. Your final transaction price may vary depending on your local mandi, buyer negotiation, and current supply conditions.

Blockchain & Transactions

Every transaction created on VEGEGRADE is anchored to the Sepolia Testnet - a blockchain network - creating a permanent, tamper-proof record. This ensures:

  • Transparent price discovery for farmers
  • Fraud prevention in grading and trade
  • An auditable trail for government oversight
  • Protection against retroactive manipulation of records

No. Once a transaction is submitted and recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be deleted or modified. This is by design to ensure data integrity. If a transaction was entered with incorrect data, please contact support with your transaction ID to flag it for review.

All your past transactions are available under the Transaction History tab. You can filter by date range, grade, and status. Each record includes the blockchain transaction hash for independent verification.
