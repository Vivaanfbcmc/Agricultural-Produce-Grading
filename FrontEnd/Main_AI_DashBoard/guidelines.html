Photo Upload Guidelines

Follow these best practices to ensure accurate AI-based grading results.

The accuracy of the AI grading system depends significantly on the quality of the image uploaded. Poor lighting, blurry photographs, or cluttered backgrounds can result in an incorrect grade. Following these guidelines will help you get the most reliable result.
Step-by-Step Upload Process
1

Prepare the Produce

Arrange a representative sample of vegetables on a clean, flat surface. Remove any loose soil or debris from the surface.

2

Set Up Lighting

Move near a window or under a bright overhead light. Ensure no strong shadows fall across the vegetables.

3

Take the Photo

Hold the camera 30-60 cm above the produce. Ensure the entire sample is in frame and the image is in focus before capturing.

4

Review & Upload

Check the photo is sharp and well-lit before uploading. Then click "Browse Files" on the AI Assessment page to submit.

5

Wait for Analysis

Do not close or refresh the page. The AI model will return results within 5-30 seconds depending on server load.

6

Create Transaction

Once graded, use the "Create Transaction" button to record the trade on the blockchain ledger.

Lighting Best Practices

Lighting is the single most important factor affecting grade accuracy. The AI model evaluates colour uniformity and surface defects - both of which are highly sensitive to lighting conditions.

✔ Good Lighting
  • Natural daylight from a window (indirect)
  • Bright, even artificial overhead light
  • LED ring light held above the produce
  • Multiple light sources to eliminate shadows
  • Soft diffused light (avoid direct harsh sun)
✘ Poor Lighting
  • Camera flash (causes glare and colour distortion)
  • Single side light creating deep shadows
  • Low-light or dim indoor conditions
  • Coloured light sources (yellow bulbs, neon lights)
  • Direct bright sunlight (overexposes the image)
Background & Arrangement
✔ Recommended
  • Plain white paper or cloth as background
  • Produce spread out, not stacked or overlapping
  • All items of the same type in one image
  • Produce takes up 60-80% of the frame
  • Camera held steady (use a stand if possible)
✘ Avoid
  • Cluttered backgrounds (gunny bags, soil, tools)
  • Produce piled in a heap or crate
  • Mixing tomatoes with other vegetables
  • Partial or cut-off items at image edges
  • Motion blur from moving the camera
Example Setups

✔ Near Window
Indirect natural light, no shadows, top-down view on white cloth.
💡
✔ Overhead LED
Bright white LED directly above. Produce spread evenly on plain surface.
📸
✘ Camera Flash
Flash causes glare, washes out colour, hides surface defects.
🌑
✘ Single Side Light
Half the produce in shadow - AI cannot assess both sides accurately.
📦
✘ Crate / Heap
Stacked produce obscures individual items. AI grades the visible surface only.
Field Tip: If you are in a field or mandi with no white background available, place the produce on a clean, dry jute sack or newspaper. Avoid wet or stained surfaces, as these can affect colour detection. Taking the photo in open shade (under a tree or canopy) gives the most consistent results outdoors.
Common Upload Errors & Solutions
Error Likely Cause Solution
File too large High-resolution photo from DSLR or modern smartphone Compress the image using any free photo editor before uploading
Unsupported format HEIC format from iPhone cameras Convert to JPG via your phone's share/export settings
Blurry result / Low confidence Out-of-focus or motion-blurred image Retake with better focus; tap the produce on your phone screen to focus
Incorrect grade (suspected) Poor lighting or cluttered background Retake under better lighting on a plain background and re-analyze
Analysis stuck / Timeout Server load or slow internet connection Wait 30 seconds, then refresh and try again
