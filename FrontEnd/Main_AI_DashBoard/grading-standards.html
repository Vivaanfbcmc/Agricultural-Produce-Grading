AI Grading Standards

How VEGEGRADE evaluates vegetable quality using computer vision and assigns grades.

Vegetables are analyzed using computer vision techniques that evaluate visual features such as colour uniformity, size consistency, surface defects, and freshness indicators. Based on these parameters, the system automatically classifies produce into Grade A, Grade B, Grade C, or Rotten categories.
How the AI Assessment Works

When you upload an image, VEGEGRADE's ML Engine passes it through a multi-stage computer vision pipeline. The model was trained on thousands of labelled vegetable images representing all grade categories and a wide range of field conditions. Assessment is completed in seconds and is fully automated - no human intervention is involved in the grading decision.

Visual Parameters Evaluated

The AI model evaluates four primary visual parameters. Each parameter is assessed independently and combined to produce an overall grade classification.

🎨

Colour Uniformity

Evaluates how consistent the colour is across the produce surface. Irregular patches, discolouration, or browning reduce the score.

📐

Size Consistency

Assesses whether the produce matches the expected size range for the commodity. Extreme undersizing or oversizing is penalised.

🔬

Surface Defects

Detects cracks, bruises, scabs, insect damage, physical injuries, and blemishes on the skin surface of the vegetable.

💧

Freshness Indicators

Identifies visual signs of wilting, shrivelling, moisture loss, and early decay that indicate reduced shelf life.

Grade Definitions & Criteria
Grade A

Excellent Quality

Premium produce - highest market value

Rs. 2,400 / qtl
Parameter Criteria
Colour Uniformity Highly uniform; rich, vibrant colour typical of the variety with no discolouration
Size Consistency Within the standard size range for the commodity; well-formed and regular shape
Surface Defects No visible cracks, bruises, or blemishes; skin is smooth and intact
Freshness Firm, plump appearance; no signs of wilting or moisture loss
Suitable for: Premium retail, supermarkets, direct consumer sale, and export. Commands highest wholesale and retail price in all major mandis.
Grade B

Good Quality

Minor imperfections - suitable for general retail

Rs. 1,800 / qtl
Parameter Criteria
Colour Uniformity Mostly uniform; minor colour variation or slight off-tone areas acceptable
Size Consistency Slight variation from ideal size acceptable; shape mostly regular
Surface Defects Minor surface blemishes or superficial marks permitted; no deep bruising
Freshness Good firmness; no significant wilting; slight skin texture change acceptable
Suitable for: Local retail markets, mandi direct sale, institutional buyers (hotels, canteens). Some buyers may grade downward; negotiate accordingly.
Grade C

Average Quality

Noticeable defects - processing and secondary markets

Rs. 1,100 / qtl
Parameter Criteria
Colour Uniformity Noticeable discolouration, yellow patches, or browning on significant surface area
Size Consistency Noticeably small, oversized, or irregular shape; significant deviation from standard
Surface Defects Visible bruises, cracks, scabs, or insect damage; more than minor surface blemishes
Freshness Some wilting, softening, or shrivelling visible; reduced shelf life
Suitable for: Industrial processing, juice and paste production, dehydration units, and price-sensitive secondary markets. Not recommended for direct retail.
Rotten

Rejected

Significant decay or damage - not accepted for trade

Not Tradeable
Parameter Criteria
Colour Uniformity Dark patches, widespread blackening, white mould growth, or severe discolouration
Size Consistency Collapsed, shrivelled, or deformed beyond recognition of original shape
Surface Defects Extensive decay, leaking fluids, mould, severe physical injury, or fungal growth
Freshness Decomposition evident; produce no longer safe for food use in any form
Action: Rotten produce is rejected by the system and cannot be recorded in a trade transaction. Segregate immediately to prevent spread of decay to Grade B/C stock.

Limitations of AI Grading

The VEGEGRADE AI model provides reliable results under standard conditions, but there are known limitations to be aware of:

  • The model assesses visual surface quality only. It cannot detect internal defects, sugar content, firmness (hardness), or odour.
  • Poor image quality - including blur, deep shadows, or cluttered backgrounds - may result in a lower grade or incorrect classification.
  • Results are advisory, not legally binding. For official certification, contact your APMC office.
  • The model is currently trained and validated for tomatoes only. Uploading images of other vegetables will produce unreliable results.
  • Grade accuracy may vary for unusual or heirloom varieties not well-represented in the training dataset.
